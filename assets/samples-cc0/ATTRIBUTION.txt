DRUMS: Manta's percussion kit, thirty one-shots (00_quinto .. 29_wash_tap_air) made by tools/ma_perc.py.
Twenty-six are taken from the Versilian Community Sample Library (VCSL, by Sam Gossner and contributors, CC0 1.0,
https://github.com/sgossner/VCSL): its Conga, Bongos, Tom 1, Tom 2, Snare Drum (Rope Tension), Cowbells, Agogo
Bells, Claves, Woodblock, Guiro, Shaker (Small), Cabasa and Triangles folders (the files are named in
tools/ma_perc.py), each summed to mono, started at its onset, cut and faded, normalised. The two timbales are the
attack of VCSL's rope-tension snare (snares off) over a tuned head and shell synthesized by tools/ma_perc.py. The
kick and the washboard (a scrape and a tap) are synthesized by tools/ma_perc.py (seeded, so it always makes the same
files): no recording in them. 16-bit mono WAV at 44.1 kHz; tools/gen_samples.py encodes them into the firmware (the
skins at 22,050 Hz, the metal, wood and shakers at 32 kHz). CC0 as their source; Manta keeps them so.
PIANO: Manta's upright, six notes (00_Upright_G1 .. 05_Upright_C4, VCSL's names: C3 is middle C) of the Yamaha
upright of VCSL ("Chordophones/Zithers/Upright Piano, Yamaha/Sustains", the vl3 layer), changed by
tools/ma_piano.py: summed to mono, cut to 0.8 .. 1.0 s and faded, a high-pass at 110 Hz, a little high shelf and
bite, a touch less body, a light firmness, normalised. CC0 as their source; Manta keeps it so.
