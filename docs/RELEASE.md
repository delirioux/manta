# Manta 0.1

The first test build: a Latin band on the FM-1.

- Twelve styles on two sides: CALIENTE (SALSA, MAMBO, MERENGUE, CUMBIA, SAMBA, GUARACHA) and NOCHE (SON, CHA-CHA,
  BOLERO, BOSSA, TANGO, DANZON), every pattern on the clave, 3-2 or 2-3.
- A sampled percussion section of thirty one-shots (congas, bongos, timbales, bells, agogô, claves, woodblock, güiro,
  shakers, cabasa, triangle, toms, a kick, a washboard), a sampled upright piano, a sampled trumpet and
  trombone section (VSCO 2 CE), the trombone an octave under the trumpets, a sampled tenor sax and violin section,
  pan pipes, and a nylon guitar that strums each style its own way (the bossa's and the samba's own) and a requinto
  for lead riffs.
- A Havana song's form: intro, tema, verso, corte, frase, piano, montuno, mambo, moña, cierre; no singing, a solo
  where a singer would be; the upright beside the guitar on the PERC track.
- REC is BAND HIT (held: a descarga), SEQ is MAMBO, the keys play the solo trumpet with its mutes (filters over the
  real horn), PAN 3D on headphones.

Not yet: the traditional songs' phrases (TRAD), Sardine's full binaural PAN 3D.

Runs on the FM-1. To go back: Felucca's or [Tuna](https://github.com/delirioux/tuna)'s installer, or the official V15.
