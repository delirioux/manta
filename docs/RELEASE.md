# Manta 1.0

A Latin band on the FM-1: generative grooves on the clave, the player leading it and taking the solo.

- Twelve styles on two sides: CALIENTE (SALSA, MAMBO, MERENGUE, CUMBIA, SAMBA, GUARACHA) and NOCHE (SON, CHA-CHA,
  BOLERO, BOSSA, TANGO, DANZON), every pattern on the clave, 3-2 or 2-3, each with its tempo, sounds and percussion.
- Sampled (CC0): thirty percussion one-shots, a Yamaha upright, a trumpet and trombone section, a tenor sax, a violin
  section and a plucked double bass; a nylon guitar, a tres and a requinto on PHYS's strings; the accordion and the
  bandoneon as FM reeds.
- A Havana song's form: intro, tema, verso, corte, frase, piano, montuno (the horns' coro and the solo by turns), mambo,
  moña, cierre; no singing, a solo where a singer would be.
- SCOPES, the home screen: every part's scope. HOME goes round PLAY and a page for each instrument (PIANO BASS HORNS
  PERC): its scope and the notes it will play; the keys play it; EDIT writes its notes over with the keys, EDIT again
  plays them, kept with the settings and in eight sets.
- REC is BAND HIT (a new offbeat cierre each tap; held, a descarga), SEQ is MAMBO, PLAY held fades the song out. The
  keys play the solo trumpet with its mutes (AUTO: a new mute each solo), slurred as a player would; the keys glow on
  the key's scale and the chord. PAN 3D on headphones.

Not yet: the traditional songs' phrases (TRAD), Sardine's full binaural PAN 3D.

Runs on the FM-1. Manta keeps its settings, sets and patterns in Felucca's first four song slots. To go back:
Felucca's or [Tuna](https://github.com/delirioux/tuna)'s installer, or the official V15.
